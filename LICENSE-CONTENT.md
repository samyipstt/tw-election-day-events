## Upstream sources and their terms

The files in `data/` compile facts from the public sources below. Every row carries its source URL or names its source.

- **Company registry** — 經濟部商工登記公示資料查詢服務 (findbiz) and the 經濟部商業發展署 company open data (GCIS):
  Taiwan government open data, 政府資料開放授權條款 (Open Government Data License, version 1.0). Director and supervisor
  names are reproduced as published by the registry.
- **Political-donation register** — 監察院 政治獻金公開查閱平台 (https://ardata.cy.gov.tw/): public register extracts.
- **Ticketing platforms and organiser pages** — 拓元 tixcraft, 遠大售票, 寬宏售票, ibon, 年代售票, KKTIX, iNDIEVOX,
  Legacy, 臺北流行音樂中心, 高雄流行音樂中心, setlist.fm: event facts (titles, dates, venues, credits) only, cited by URL.
- **zh.wikipedia.org** 〈臺北小巨蛋演唱會列表〉 (CC BY-SA 4.0): dates and titles of past Taipei Arena concerts.
  Only facts (date, title) are taken from it; no Wikipedia text is reproduced, and nothing from it is re-licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- **ETtoday 新聞雲** entertainment news lists, retrieved via the **Internet Archive Wayback Machine**: titles, dates and URLs
  only; no article text is redistributed.
- **中央選舉委員會 / 中央社**: polling dates.

Facts are not subject to copyright in most jurisdictions; this file records provenance and attribution.
